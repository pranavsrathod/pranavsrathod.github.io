## Hey, I’m Pranav 👋

I’m a creative technologist, engineer, and sometimes a quiet observer of the world — currently pursuing my **Master’s in Computer Science** at the **University of Southern California**, specializing in **Multimedia and Creative Technologies**.

The semester just wrapped up, but I’m still deeply interested in **Computer Animation and Simulation** and **Game Engine Development**. For the summer, I’m diving into learning and experimenting with **Touch Designer** — exploring new ways to blend real-time graphics, interactivity, and creativity. I’m also squeezing in some coding and creative projects on the side!

When I’m not coding or debugging, I’m probably solving LeetCode problems or editing videos in Premiere Pro — trying to earn that certification and level up both technically and creatively.

### What I Care About

I’m inspired by urban life, identity, and internal conflict — the things that aren’t always loud but still shape how we move through the world. I love building projects that live at the intersection of emotion and interaction and has driven me to work on projects like city renderers, VR experiences, and mobile tools that mix logic with creativity.

Right now, I’m especially interested in:

- Real-time rendering & interactive storytelling  
- XR interfaces and perception  
- Systems-level design (especially in games)  
- Capturing things that make people *pause* — even for a second

### Beyond Code

I shoot photos mostly on my iPhone — framing moments from cities I live in or pass through. The plan is to graduate to a proper camera soon (open to recs if you’ve got one). My lens tends to gravitate toward movement, symmetry, and the small dramas of everyday life.

One city that’s particularly close to my heart is Chicago. Its stunning architecture, lively streets, and diverse culinary scene have shaped much of my inspiration in art, food, and urban life. The city’s ability to embrace the extremes — from icy winters to celebratory summers — taught me resilience and how communities come together to make the most of every moment. Chicago also helped me appreciate the small joys, like a perfect summer day when the whole city feels alive.
